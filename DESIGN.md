# Design System Direction

## Scene

This product is used in dim rooms, often while a film is actively playing. The interface should feel like a calm but high-precision screening console: cinematic, tactile, legible at a glance, and more editorial than dashboard-generic.

## Visual direction

- Dark committed palette, not light SaaS
- Deep ink and ember as the identity anchor
- Cool cyan reserved for live / sensing / motion data
- Warm gold reserved for highlighted moments and film metadata
- Strong typography hierarchy with a serif display voice and a cleaner operational body face

## Product principles

- The timeline is the hero, not summary cards.
- Pairing should feel operational and trustworthy, not form-heavy.
- “Intensity” must read as a continuous signal, not a gimmick score.
- The web and watch should feel like the same system, adapted to different constraints.

## Watch principles

- Venu-first readability with high contrast and minimal text density
- Clear phase changes: idle, primed, recording, uploading, complete
- Metrics should be visible in one glance during playback
- Controls should stay simple enough for single-button confirmation
