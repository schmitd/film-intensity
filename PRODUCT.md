# Film Intensity

## Product

Film Intensity is a Garmin-first second-screen product that lets viewers record heart rate, stress, and HRV-derived biometric shifts during a movie, then maps those signals to the exact moments of the film timeline.

The product has two surfaces:

- A Garmin Connect IQ watch app that pairs to a film session, records biometrics during playback, and uploads batched session data.
- A web application that creates sessions, manages pairing, and visualizes which films and moments generate the strongest physiological response across viewers.

## Audience

- Early adopters who already wear Garmin watches and enjoy movies as events
- Small pilot groups running screenings at home or with friends
- Eventually, film communities comparing “most intense moment” fingerprints across titles

## Primary user outcome

A user should be able to:

1. choose a film on the web
2. pair a Garmin watch with a short code
3. record a full movie session
4. see a crowd-readable intensity curve and ranked peak moments afterward

## Current v1 scope

- Direct watch-to-backend upload
- Small supported Garmin set, centered on Venu watches
- Light-account or pilot-account model
- Preselected film before recording
- Entertainment analytics, not medical inference
